# ScoreNine — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

## 1. Who we are

ScoreNine is a YouTube channel (@ScoreNine) publishing daily quiz Shorts. The videos are produced and published with automated software operated by the channel owner ("we", "us").

## 2. What we access

To publish videos and manage the channel, our software uses the official YouTube Data API with the channel owner's own Google account, with these permissions:

- Upload videos to the channel
- Manage the channel's own comments
- Read the channel's own statistics

We do NOT access, collect, or store any data about viewers or subscribers.

## 3. Viewer data

We do not collect personal data from viewers. Comments posted on our videos are public YouTube content governed by YouTube's own Terms and Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy). We read and reply to comments within YouTube only and do not export them elsewhere.

## 4. Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share any data with third parties. No analytics tools beyond YouTube Studio are used.

## 5. Revoking access

The channel owner can revoke the software's access at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
